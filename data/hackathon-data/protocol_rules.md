# Clinical Trial Protocol: ATLAS-001 Rules & Criteria

## 1. Study Schedule of Activities
- Screening / Visit 1: Day -14 to Day 1
- Visit 2: Day 14 (+/- 2 days)
- Visit 3: Day 28 (+/- 3 days)
- Visit 4 / End of Study: Day 84 (+/- 5 days)

Visits conducted outside these designated allowable windows must be reported as Protocol Deviations.

## 2. Drug-Induced Liver Injury (Hy's Law) Criteria
A subject qualifies as a potential Hy's Law candidate if:
1. Alanine Aminotransferase (ALT) > 3x Upper Limit of Normal (ULN), OR Aspartate Aminotransferase (AST) > 3x ULN, AND
2. Total Bilirubin > 2x ULN, AND
3. No initial evidence of cholestasis or biliary obstruction.

Action: Study drug must be immediately interrupted or discontinued, and hepatology consultation requested.

## 3. Dose Adjustment Guidelines
- Grade 1 AE: Maintain study drug dose.
- Grade 2 AE: Optional dose reduction (50mg -> 25mg).
- Grade 3 or Severe AE: Mandatory dose interruption or reduction.
- Grade 4 / Life-threatening AE: Permanent discontinuation.

## 4. Laboratory Quantitation Limits
- Viral Load RNA: Lower limit of quantitation (LLOQ) is 5 copies/mL. Values reported as '<5' denote non-detectable or below quantitation limit and must NOT be treated as zero or compared mathematically to standard numerical thresholds.
