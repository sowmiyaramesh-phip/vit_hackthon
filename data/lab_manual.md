# Laboratory Manual & Unit Harmonization Guide

## 1. Unit Standards & Conversions
- Total Bilirubin:
  - Standard US: mg/dL
  - SI Units (Site 102): umol/L
  - Conversion: 1 mg/dL = 17.1 umol/L
- Creatinine:
  - Standard US: mg/dL
  - SI Units (Site 103): umol/L
  - Conversion: 1 mg/dL = 88.4 umol/L

## 2. Site Laboratory Reference Ranges
Reference ranges vary across investigation centers due to local analytical assays. All lab results must be evaluated against the site-specific range defined in 
eference_ranges.csv.
